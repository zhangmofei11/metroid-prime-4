Chapter-by-chapter guide with all objectives and hidden areas.

CHAPTER 1: AWAKENING

Starport → Aurora Basin

Tutorial
Duration:1-2 hours
Power-ups:3/8
Difficulty:Easy

Objectives:

  • • Learn basic movement and scanning
  • • Acquire the Scan Visor
  • • Navigate the crashed frigate
  • • Find the first Energy Tank
  • • Defeat the Space Pirate scout (mini-boss)
02

CHAPTER 2: THE DARK WORLD

Aurora Basin → Dark Aether

Main Story
Duration:3-4 hours
Power-ups:5/15
Difficulty:Normal

Objectives:

  • • Portal to Dark Aether opens
  • • Acquire the Dark Suit
  • • Navigate the Toxic Swamp
  • • Find the Dark Beam weapon
  • • Defeat the Ing Guardian (mid-boss)
03

CHAPTER 3: SANCTUARY

Sky Temple → Sanctuary Fortress

Critical
Duration:4-5 hours
Power-ups:8/25
Difficulty:Hard

Objectives:

  • • Enter the Sky Temple
  • • Solve the light puzzle sequences
  • • Acquire the Annihilator Beam
  • • Face the Temple Guardian (boss)
  • • Retrieve the Sky Temple Key
04

CHAPTER 4: THE FINAL DARK

Emperor's Throne → Dark Aether Core

Finale
Duration:3-4 hours
Power-ups:10/35
Difficulty:Very Hard

Objectives:

  • • Confront the Emperor Ing
  • • Use all beam combinations effectively
  • • Destroy the Dark Splinter spawners
  • • Final battle: Dark Samus returns
  • • Save the Luminoth from extinction
