⚡ COMBINATION BEAMS

Dark + Light = Sonic

Creates sonic waves that penetrate walls. Essential for hidden areas in Sky Temple. Stuns enemies through obstacles.

Power + Annihilator = Nova

Massive explosive damage in small radius. Use against groups and bosses during phase transitions.
