BOSS GUIDE

Complete boss strategies with attack patterns and weaknesses.

SPACE PIRATE SCOUT

Chapter 1 - Tutorial Boss

HP400
Easy

Attack Patterns

Plasma Shot

Single plasma projectile. Simple dodge sideways.

Charge Rush

Melee charge attack. Back dodge to avoid.

Strategy

Tutorial boss. Stay mobile, use missiles when openings appear. Scan for lore entry. No special mechanics.

ING GUARDIAN

Chapter 2 - Dark Aether Boss

HP2,200
Normal

Attack Patterns

Shadow Strike

Teleports and strikes. Track影子, dodge at flash.

Dark Tentacles

Ground-based attack. Jump to avoid.

Phase 2 - Rage

Faster attacks, spawns Inglets. Use Dark Beam.

Strategy

• Use Dark Beam for primary damage

• Phase 2: Switch to Power Beam for consistent DPS

• Watch for ground telegraph before tentacles

• Safe windows after each attack combo

DARK SAMUS

Chapter 4 - Final Boss

HP6,000
Extreme

Attack Patterns

Phazon Beam

Charged shot with tracking. Strafe while charging.

Morph Ball Bomb

Underground attack. Jump to avoid explosions.

Phazon Splits

Creates 3 copies. Shoot real one with Scan Visor.

Phase 2 - Counter

Parry her attacks with your own beam clash.

Strategy

• Use all beam types - switch based on phase

• Watch for Phazon Splits - scan immediately

• Phase 2: Counter attack when beams clash

• Keep moving - she teleports frequently

• Missiles during vulnerability windows

Recommended: All beams upgraded, 10+ Energy Tanks, Phase Grip upgrade
