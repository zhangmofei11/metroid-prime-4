TIPS & TRICKS

Advanced strategies and hidden secrets.

B BEGINNER TIPS

1

Scan Everything

The Scan Visor reveals lore, weak points, and hidden paths. Always scan new enemies and environments.

2

Explore Backtrack Areas

New abilities open old areas. After getting Spider Ball, return to Chapter 1 zones for missed items.

3

Energy Management

Never go into boss fights below 5 Energy Tanks. Health pickups are rare in boss arenas.

4

Use Morph Ball Often

Many secrets are hidden in small passages. If a tunnel looks too small, use Morph Ball anyway.

A ADVANCED STRATEGIES

Beam Switching

Switch beams based on enemy type. Dark enemies take extra from Light Beam. Armored enemies weak to charged Plasma.

Boss Weakness Exploit

Every boss has a scan-revealed weakness. Watch for glowing weak points and switch to the matching beam element.

Missile Management

Save missiles for boss phases and heavy enemies. Regular combat is more ammo-efficient with Power Beam.

Backtrack Rewards

End game requires 100% items. Don't skip any zones - many Missile and Energy expansions are only accessible with late-game abilities.

? HIDDEN SECRETS

🔍

Secret Docks

In Sky Temple, bomb the wall behind the main altar. Leads to area with 3 Energy Tanks and lore about Luminoth civilization.

🎭

Phazon Suit Secret

The Phazon Suit isn't just for protection. In Emperor's Throne, it reveals invisible platforms leading to secret cache.

🏆

Completionist Reward

100% completion unlocks Concept Art gallery and "True Hero" ending with extended cutscene showing Samus's future.
