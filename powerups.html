SUITS & POWER-UPS

All abilities, suits, and expansions with locations.

🦾 SUITS

P

Power Suit

Default Suit

Starting suit. Basic protection and mobility.

V

Varia Suit

Heat Resistant

Found: Aurora Basin. Protects from lava and extreme heat.

G

Gravity Suit

Underwater Mobility

Found: Sky Temple. Enables full movement in water and anti-gravity.

P

Phazon Suit

Phazon Resistant

Found: Emperor's Throne. Required for final area. Phazon damage reduced.

⚡ ABILITIES

Morph Ball

Default

Roll through small tunnels. Required for many secret areas.

Spider Ball

Chapter 2

Attach to magnetic surfaces. Essential for vertical progression.

Boost Ball

Chapter 2

Charge and boost while morphed. Opens speed tunnels.

Grapple Beam

Chapter 3

Swing across gaps and pull objects. Multi-use tool in puzzles.

Seeker Missile

Chapter 3

Lock onto 5 targets simultaneously. Essential for certain bosses.

Space Jump

Chapter 4

Infinite spin jumps in mid-air. Opens access to late-game areas.
